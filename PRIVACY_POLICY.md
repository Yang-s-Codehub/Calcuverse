## 2. Information We Collect

We collect two broad categories of information: (a) information that remains only on your device, and (b) information collected automatically by third-party services integrated into the App.

### 2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is created and stored **only on your device** and is **not transmitted to us**:

- **Calculation history** — the expressions you enter, the results produced, and a timestamp for each entry.
- **App preferences and settings** — for example, theme, calculator mode, unit preferences, tool configurations, and layout choices.
- **Floating calculator settings** — window position, size, and opacity for the on-screen floating calculator overlay.
- **Currency exchange rates cache** — a local copy of publicly available exchange rates (see Section 3.3).

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the calculation history in the App, clearing the App's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the App.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

When you use the App, the following third-party services may collect information automatically. We do not directly store this data on our own servers; it is processed by these providers under their own privacy policies.

| Category | Data | Collected by | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Advertising | Advertising identifier (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, device type, OS version, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction data | Google AdMob | To display and measure banner and interstitial advertisements |
| Consent management | Your privacy/consent choices, device and region information | Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK | To obtain and store your consent for personalized advertising where required by law |
| Analytics | App usage events, sessions, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP, advertising identifier (where permitted) | Google Firebase Analytics | To understand aggregate usage and improve the App |
| Crash reporting | Crash stack traces, device state and model, OS version, app version, app instance identifiers | Google Firebase Crashlytics | To diagnose and fix crashes and improve stability |
| Performance monitoring | App startup and trace performance data, device and app information | Google Firebase Performance Monitoring | To monitor and improve App performance |
| Purchases | Purchase and transaction status for in-app purchases and subscriptions (no full payment card data) | Google Play Billing (and Apple App Store / store services on Apple platforms) | To process and validate in-app purchases and subscriptions |

Information collected by these third parties may include identifiers that are considered personal information under certain laws (such as your advertising identifier and IP address). We do **not** receive your name, email address, or precise physical location, and we do not combine third-party data with your calculation content.

## 3. How We Use Information

We and our third-party providers use information for the following purposes:

1. **To provide the App's core functionality** — performing calculations, saving history and settings locally, and enabling tools and the floating calculator.
2. **To display advertisements** — showing banner and interstitial ads, including interest-based advertising where you have consented.
3. **To obtain and honor your consent preferences** — complying with consent requirements in your region.
4. **To analyze usage and improve the App** — understanding which features are used and improving usability and performance.
5. **To diagnose and fix crashes and performance issues** — ensuring the App is stable and fast.
6. **To process purchases** — enabling and validating premium features through subscriptions and one-time purchases.
7. **To comply with legal obligations** — responding to lawful requests and enforcing our terms.

### 3.1 Legal Bases (for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Consent** — for personalized advertising and certain analytics/cookies, where required. You may withdraw consent at any time via the in-app privacy settings.
- **Legitimate interests** — for essential functionality, security, fraud prevention, and aggregate analytics/crash reporting that are necessary to operate and improve the App, balanced against your rights.
- **Contract** — for processing purchases you initiate.
- **Legal obligation** — where we must comply with applicable law.

### 3.2 Currency Exchange Rates

The App retrieves daily reference exchange rates published by the **European Central Bank (ECB)** and caches them locally on your device to allow offline use. These requests do not include your calculation data. Please refer to the ECB's own policies for any information about their servers.

### 3.3 Network Access

The App requests network access solely to fetch exchange rates, load advertisements, send analytics/crash/performance data, and process purchases. The App does not transmit your calculation expressions, results, or history over the network.
